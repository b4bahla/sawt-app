    What the web version can and cannot do

    Works: recording with Sawt open on screen, importing audio and video, Arabic and English transcription, key points, minutes, tasks, and export to Word, PDF, Markdown, text, and Notes.

    Does not: keep recording when you lock the phone or switch apps; iOS stops web apps from using the microphone in the background. Phone calls cannot be recorded by any app; record them with the Phone app and import the file here.

    Sawt works entirely offline after setup. Recordings, transcripts, and summaries stay on this iPhone. There is no account.
